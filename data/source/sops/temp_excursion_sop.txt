Standard Operating Procedure: Cargo Temperature Excursion
1. Trigger: If cargo temperature exceeds 8.0°C for refrigerated items or drops below -20.0°C for deep freeze items for more than 15 consecutive minutes.
2. Immediate Actions:
   a. Notify the driver immediately to verify cooling unit power and door seal integrity.
   b. Dispatcher checks auxiliary power telemetry and compressor error codes.
3. Escalation:
   a. If temperature remains above critical threshold after 30 minutes, re-route vehicle to the nearest secondary cold storage depot.
   b. Log an emergency cargo inspection report upon arrival.
