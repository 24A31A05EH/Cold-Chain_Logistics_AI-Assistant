Standard Operating Procedure: Vehicle Mechanical & Engine Overheating
1. Trigger: Engine coolant temperature exceeds 105°C or speed drops below 10 km/h on active transit corridors.
2. Immediate Actions:
   a. Command vehicle to pull over safely into the nearest rest stop or hard shoulder.
   b. Activate auxiliary refrigeration backup generator to preserve cold chain payload independently of engine status.
3. Recovery:
   a. Request mobile emergency repair unit or dispatch replacement tractor unit to swap trailer within 2 hours.
