Standard Operating Procedure: Route Delays and Port Congestion
1. Trigger: ETA variance exceeds 45 minutes or port congestion status is marked CRITICAL/HIGH.
2. Immediate Actions:
   a. Review secondary and tertiary bypass routes to circumvent choke points.
   b. Notify destination distribution center manager regarding updated delivery window.
3. Rerouting:
   a. Recalculate fuel economy and route risk score before confirming route change with the driver.
